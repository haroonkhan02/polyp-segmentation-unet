Polyp segmentation in your browser

A U-Net (ResNet-34 encoder) trained on Kvasir-SEG colonoscopy frames, quantised to INT8 and run with onnxruntime-web. The model downloads once (25 MB) and every image is processed on your device; nothing is uploaded.

Research demo. Not a medical device and not for clinical use.

Uncertainty is Monte-Carlo dropout: a second model file (25 MB, loaded when you open that view) runs the decoder 8 times with dropout on and shows the per-pixel standard deviation. It flags uncertain boundaries well but can miss whole regions the model is confidently wrong about; examples 4 and 5 are two of the worst test cases.

Trained on 800 frames from one hospital (Kvasir-SEG, Jha et al. 2020, CC BY 4.0); the example frames come from its held-out test split. Numbers, training code and the error analysis: GitHub repo.
